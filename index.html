About FreshFind Platform

Connecting communities with local growers and fresh seasonal produce

Background & Necessity

Farmers markets play a crucial role in connecting communities with local growers. However, information about operating days, hours, and available seasonal produce is frequently scattered across printed flyers, social media posts, and word-of-mouth.

Our Purpose

FreshFind is designed to help people easily find local farmers markets, check their operating hours, and discover fresh seasonal produce.

Our Team

FreshFind is developed by KINETICODE, a student team interested in web development, technology, and creating useful digital solutions for the community.

Our Platform

FreshFind is a responsive web platform that provides market information, seasonal produce details, search, bookmarks, and a simple help assistant in one place.
